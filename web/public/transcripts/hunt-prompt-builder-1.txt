
[1m  Hunting hypotheses that can fail[0m

[36m  service-accounts[0m
      Service accounts are being used interactively from infrastructure we
      do not own.
[2m      why:    Mission 1. Service accounts have a narrow, machine-shaped baseline, so deviation is unusually legible.[0m
[2m      tables: SigninLogs, AADNonInteractiveUserSignInLogs, AuditLogs, BehaviorAnalytics[0m

[36m  oauth-consent[0m
      One or more users granted OAuth consent to an unverified publisher,
      and that application then read mail or files within the hour.
[2m      why:    Mission 2. Consent grants are quiet, survive password resets via offline_access, and rarely have a matching analytics rule.[0m
[2m      tables: AuditLogs, CloudAppEvents, BehaviorAnalytics, IdentityInfo[0m

[36m  impossible-travel[0m
      An account signed in from two locations too far apart for the
      elapsed time, from an ASN outside our known egress ranges.
[2m      why:    The classic. The ASN clause is what separates a real hunt from a VPN false-positive generator.[0m
[2m      tables: SigninLogs, BehaviorAnalytics, IdentityInfo[0m

[36m  lateral-movement[0m
      A host authenticated to five or more distinct hosts within ten
      minutes using an account with no prior logon to any of them.
[2m      why:    Names an observable, not a tactic. 'Look for lateral movement' is not a hypothesis.[0m
[2m      tables: DeviceLogonEvents, IdentityLogonEvents, BehaviorAnalytics[0m

[36m  inbox-rules[0m
      An inbox rule was created that forwards or deletes mail matching
      finance keywords, on a mailbox whose owner did not create a rule in
      the previous 90 days.
[2m      why:    Business email compromise leaves this trace almost every time, and it is usually below alerting thresholds.[0m
[2m      tables: CloudAppEvents, OfficeActivity, EmailEvents[0m

[36m  ueba-orphans[0m
      UEBA anomaly clusters exist in the last 7 days with no matching
      incident, indicating behaviour no analytics rule covers.
[2m      why:    Mission 2 step 1. Starting from anomalies instead of alerts is the flip that finds what the rules missed.[0m
[2m      tables: BehaviorAnalytics, SecurityIncident, SecurityAlert[0m
